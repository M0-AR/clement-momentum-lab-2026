🍌 Donkey-proof story (read this if nothing else)

Imagine a donkey carrying gold up a hill. 1) Weather first: if storm (market down), donkey rests in barn — no gold lost. That is the regime filter (QQQ trend). 2) Pick strong donkeys: only fast, healthy ones (liquid, jumpy +3%/day, rising 50/200-day). 3) Wait for crouch-then-jump: price squeezes quiet (low volume) then jumps (breakout) — you ride. Or small dip to the path (pullback) — you hop on cheap. 4) Tiny backpacks: risk 30 cents per $100. Even 10 losses = $3. You never die. 5) Take some gold early: sell 1/3 when happy (+2.5× wiggle), let rest run, quit if path breaks. 6) Monthly rule: if down 5% this month, carry feathers until you see a perfect hill. That is the whole repo. Everything else is details.
